# Rocci knowledge

Type-first product work archive with area nesting. Current contracts live in
Architecture; dated shipped-or-not snapshots live in Status. Do not flatten
product areas or rename concept IDs to answer “what is current?”

Current-map questions:

* What is the current product contract? → [System overview](architecture/system-overview.md) (five architecture records)
* What language, Rocdown, docs, and theme contracts are current? → [Language tooling](architecture/language-tooling.md), [Rocdown format](architecture/rocdown-format.md), [Rocdown documentation generator](architecture/rocdown-documentation-compiler.md), [Theming](architecture/theming.md)
* What is shipped, and what is deliberately incomplete? → [Implementation](status/implementation.md), [Known limitations](status/known-limitations.md)

Type collections:

* [Architecture](architecture/) - Current system contracts and boundaries.
* [Decisions](decisions/) - Implemented and approved choices with their consequences.
* [Status](status/) - Dated implementation state and known limitations.
* [Plans](plans/) - Active and historical implementation plans, grouped by product area (not by lifecycle).
* [Research](research/) - Reserved for non-normative evidence and synthesis.
* [Audits](audits/) - Reserved for findings against current behavior.
* [Case studies](case-studies/) - Reserved for concrete boundary examples.
* [Reference](reference/) - Migration and lookup material.
* [Design](design/) - Current design-system evidence and DTCG-informed research, explicitly separated from implementation authority.

The [Phase 2 migration matrix](migration-matrix.tsv) accounts for every section heading in the nine reports and plans that were at the repository root during migration. Phase 6 dispositions are recorded in the [consolidation reference](reference/consolidation.md).
